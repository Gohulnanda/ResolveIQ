## Summary
Participants cannot hear the user, the camera does not work in meetings, screen sharing shows a black screen, or chat messages are delayed.

## Symptoms
Colleagues report they cannot hear the user even though the headset works elsewhere. The camera shows a black picture in the meeting app. Screen sharing is blank. Teams shows a banner that messages may be delayed.

## Likely causes
- The wrong microphone or speaker selected in the app settings.
- Another application is holding the microphone or camera.
- Operating system privacy settings block the app from the device.
- An outdated client or a service incident.

## Resolution steps
1. In the meeting app device settings, select the correct microphone, speaker and camera, and run the test call.
2. Close other apps that may be using the camera or microphone.
3. Check that the operating system privacy settings allow the meeting app to use the microphone and camera.
4. Unplug and replug the headset, or try a different USB port.
5. Sign out and back in, then fully quit and restart the app; update it if an update is pending.
6. For a blank shared screen, share a single window instead of the entire screen and restart the app.

## Escalate when
Many users have audio or chat problems at the same time, or the vendor service health page reports an incident.
