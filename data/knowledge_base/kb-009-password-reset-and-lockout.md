## Summary
A user cannot sign in because the password is forgotten or expired, or the account is locked after failed attempts.

## Symptoms
The sign-in page rejects a password the user believes is correct. A message says the account is locked or that the password has expired. The self-service reset email never arrives.

## Likely causes
- Too many failed sign-in attempts triggering a temporary lock.
- A password expiry policy.
- An old password cached on a phone or a mapped drive that keeps retrying.
- A mistyped username or the wrong keyboard layout.

## Resolution steps
1. Wait about fifteen minutes if the message says the account is temporarily locked, then try again once.
2. Use the self-service password reset page and check the junk folder for the reset email.
3. Sign out of other devices and update the saved password on your phone and email app to stop repeated failures.
4. Confirm the username format and that Caps Lock is off.
5. Choose a new password that meets the length and complexity rules and has not been used before.
6. If self-service does not work, contact the service desk and verify your identity for a manual reset.

## Escalate when
Many lockouts occur without the user typing a wrong password, which can indicate a credential attack, or the reset service is unavailable.
