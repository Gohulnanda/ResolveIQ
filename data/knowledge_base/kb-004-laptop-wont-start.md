## Summary
A computer does not start, restarts in a loop, shows a blue screen, or stays black after power on.

## Symptoms
Pressing the power button does nothing, or the power light comes on with a black display. The system restarts repeatedly or shows a stop code. A desktop gives a series of beeps at startup.

## Likely causes
- A discharged battery or a failed charger or power outlet.
- Residual charge or a peripheral blocking startup.
- Corrupted system files after an interrupted update.
- Failing memory, storage or another internal component.

## Resolution steps
1. Check the outlet and charger, try another charger if available, and look for the charging light.
2. Unplug the charger and all peripherals, hold the power button for fifteen seconds, then reconnect only the charger and power on.
3. For a black screen, connect an external monitor to see whether only the display is faulty.
4. If a blue screen appears, photograph the stop code and the text and restart once.
5. Boot into recovery options and run startup repair if the system offers it.
6. Record any beep pattern or error lights for the technician.

## Escalate when
The device shows no sign of power with a known-good charger, the stop code returns after a restart, or there is physical damage such as a cracked screen.
