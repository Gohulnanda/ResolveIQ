## Summary
A printer appears offline, jobs sit in the queue without printing, or the device reports an error.

## Symptoms
The queue shows jobs as pending or an error state. The computer says the printer is offline although it is powered on. The printer panel shows a jam, toner or paper message.

## Likely causes
- A stuck job blocking the queue.
- The printer lost its network connection or has a changed address.
- A paper jam, an empty tray or low toner.
- The wrong printer selected or a missing driver.

## Resolution steps
1. Read the message on the printer panel and clear any paper jam, refill paper or replace toner as directed.
2. Power the printer off and on and wait until it reports ready.
3. Open the print queue, cancel stuck jobs, and print a single test page.
4. Confirm that the correct printer is selected and set it as default if needed.
5. Remove and re-add the printer from the software center if jobs still fail.
6. Try printing from a second computer to tell whether the printer or your computer is at fault.

## Escalate when
The printer is unreachable from every computer, shows a hardware error code after a restart, or shared printers for a whole floor are affected.
