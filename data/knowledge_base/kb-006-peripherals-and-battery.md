## Summary
External devices are not recognised, a monitor shows no signal, or a laptop runs hot and loses charge quickly.

## Symptoms
A USB device, headset, webcam or docking station is not detected. An external monitor stays blank. Keys or the mouse stop responding. The fan runs loudly, the laptop shuts down from heat, or the battery drains within an hour.

## Likely causes
- A loose cable, damaged port or faulty device.
- A missing driver or a stalled USB controller.
- Blocked ventilation or heavy background workload.
- A worn battery or aggressive power settings.

## Resolution steps
1. Reconnect the device to a different port and test it on another computer to see whether the device itself is faulty.
2. For a monitor, check the cable and the input source setting, then press the display-switch shortcut.
3. Restart the computer with the dock or device connected after a full shutdown.
4. Place the laptop on a hard flat surface, clear the vents and check which applications use the most CPU.
5. Review the battery report and reduce screen brightness or background apps.
6. Install pending firmware and driver updates from the software center.

## Escalate when
The battery report shows greatly reduced capacity, ports are physically damaged, or the device shuts down repeatedly from heat.
